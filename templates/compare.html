Summary

AES is the fastest symmetric cipher for text encryption, with minimal memory usage and very low time consumption. RSA is slower due to large-prime modular exponentiation, but enables secure key exchange without a shared secret. SHA-256 is the fastest of the three for text — it only hashes (no encryption), making it ideal for integrity checks and password storage. These results are consistent with published research on symmetric vs. asymmetric algorithm performance benchmarking.
