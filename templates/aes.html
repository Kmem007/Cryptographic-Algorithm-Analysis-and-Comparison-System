AES Encryption / Decryption

AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) is a symmetric encryption algorithm where the same secret key is used for both encryption and decryption.

How to Perform AES Encryption and Decryption

  1. Enter the plaintext message to encrypt or ciphertext to decrypt.
  2. Enter a secret key (must be the same for encryption and decryption).
  3. Click Encrypt to convert plaintext into ciphertext.
  4. Click Decrypt to convert ciphertext back into plaintext.
